🛠️ Builds & Skill Trees

Optimized character builds for Emma & Koo — stat allocation, skill priorities, and equipment recommendations for every playstyle

Parry Master

TIER S
Defensive High Skill Boss Killer

The premier build for players who can consistently execute perfect parries. Maximizes Bloom Gauge generation, keeping Koo's abilities available almost constantly. Highest DPS potential in the game when played optimally.

Stat Allocation (Priority Order)

🛡30%Stamina
🌟40%Bloom
⚔30%Strength

Key Skills

  • Emma: Extended Parry Window, Counter Slash, Parry→FP Conversion, Riposte Mastery
  • Koo: Bloom Efficiency, Quick Command, Chain Bind, Bloom Overflow

Recommended Equipment

  • Weapon: Any katana with "Parry Damage" bonus
  • Armor: Light armor — you're parrying, not tanking
  • Koo Talisman: Parry Window Extension talisman (from Kunai rematch)
  • Spirit Stones: Parry Window I, Bloom II, FP Regen I
✅ Pros: Highest DPS potential, near-infinite Bloom Gauge, trivializes humanoid bosses
❌ Cons: Highest skill requirement, struggles against unblockable-heavy bosses, fragile if you miss parries

Bloom Arts Nuker

TIER S
Offensive Burst AOE

The glass cannon approach. Max Bloom stat for devastating special attacks that can one-shot elite enemies and chunk boss HP bars. Combine with Koo's debuff abilities to maximize Bloom Art damage.

Stat Allocation (Priority Order)

🌟55%Bloom
⚔30%Strength
🛡15%Stamina

Key Skills

  • Emma: All Bloom Arts (collect from bosses!), Bloom Mastery, FP Max+, Bloom Art Combo
  • Koo: Defense Break, Elemental Weakness Exploit, Bloom Art Amplify

Recommended Equipment

  • Weapon: Katana with "Bloom Art Damage +X%"
  • Armor: Any — you're going to dodge everything anyway
  • Koo Talisman: Bloom Gauge Capacity talisman
  • Spirit Stones: Bloom III, Damage Link I, FP Regen II
✅ Pros: Massive burst damage, excellent AOE clearing, melts boss phases
❌ Cons: Very fragile, high FP dependency, weak between Bloom Art cooldowns

Ranged Sniper

TIER A
Ranged Safe Flying Enemies

Focus on ranged weapons with auto-aim enabled. Safe DPS from distance while Koo provides crowd control. Excellent for players who prefer a more strategic, less twitch-based playstyle.

Stat Allocation (Priority Order)

⚔45%Strength
🛡30%Stamina
🌟25%Bloom

Key Skills

  • Emma: Bow Mastery, Arrow Types, Auto-Aim Precision, Charged Shot
  • Koo: Binding, Slow Field, Distraction, Barrier

Recommended Equipment

  • Weapon: Hunter's Bow → Sky Piercer (endgame)
  • Armor: Medium armor for survivability
  • Koo Talisman: Aggro generation talisman (Koo draws enemy attention)
  • Spirit Stones: Vitality II, Parry Window I (for emergencies), Damage Link I
✅ Pros: Very safe playstyle, destroys flying enemies, easy to execute
❌ Cons: Lower DPS than melee builds, situational against fast enemies, ammo management

Blighthound Tank

TIER A
Defensive Beginner Friendly Survival

Maximum survivability. Stack Stamina for high HP, use heavy armor, and let Koo handle most of the damage. Perfect for newcomers to action RPGs or players who prefer a less punishing experience.

Stat Allocation (Priority Order)

🛡55%Stamina
🌟25%Bloom
⚔20%Strength

Key Skills

  • Emma: HP Boost, Guard Stability, Auto-Recovery, Second Wind
  • Koo: Healing Howl, Protective Barrier, HP Share, Revive
✅ Pros: Very forgiving, hard to die, great for learning boss patterns
❌ Cons: Lowest DPS, long fights, relies heavily on Koo for damage
