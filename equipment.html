Farming Routes

Best Farming Locations

Iron Citadel (Dungeon)

  • Drops: Ironhide Scales, Heavy armor mats
  • Recommended GS: 400+
  • Reset timer: 30 min
  • Tip: Skip trash mobs; boss drops are what you want

Astral Court (Dungeon)

  • Drops: Staff of Forgotten Stars, Astral Veil pieces
  • Recommended GS: 380+
  • Reset timer: 30 min
  • Tip: High magic resist recommended

Molten Core (World Boss)

  • Drops: Emberheart Catalyst, Eternal Ember
  • Recommended GS: 420+
  • Spawn timer: 2 hours
  • Tip: Bring fire resist potions — the DOT hurts

Elder Horned Stalker (World Boss)

  • Drops: Predator's Mark, Windstalker pieces
  • Recommended GS: 400+
  • Spawn timer: 2 hours
  • Tip: Dodge sideways, not backwards — its charge tracks

Gear Enhancement Tips

  1. Priority order: Weapon → Armor → Accessories → Artifacts
  2. Enhancement cap: +15 is the max. +10 to +15 requires Star Essence (rare drop from dungeons)
  3. Transfer enhancements: You can transfer enhancement levels between same-type items at the Blacksmith for a gold fee
  4. Sockets: Unlock sockets with Gem Chisels (dungeon drop). Insert gems for extra stats
  5. Awakening: At +15, weapons can be Awakened once, granting an additional unique passive — choose wisely, it cannot be changed
